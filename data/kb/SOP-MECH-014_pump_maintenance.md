# SOP-MECH-014: Centrifugal Pump Maintenance and Shutdown Authorisation
Revision 6 | Mechanical Maintenance Department | Effective 01 April 2026

## 1. Scope
Applies to all centrifugal pumps in the Crude Distillation, Vacuum
Distillation and Hydrotreating units.

## 2. Shutdown authorisation levels
Authorisation depends on assessed criticality:

- LOW criticality: Shift Engineer may authorise, notify Unit Head within 24 h.
- MEDIUM criticality: Unit Head authorises. Maintenance Planner to be copied.
- HIGH criticality: Requires joint approval by the Unit Head and the Head of
  Mechanical Maintenance. Where wall thickness is below the retirement limit,
  the Inspection Department must also endorse before shutdown is scheduled.

## 3. Wall thickness criteria
Measured thickness below the design minimum places equipment outside its
pressure envelope. Such equipment must not remain in service at design
conditions. A fitness-for-service assessment per API 579 is mandatory before
any decision to continue operating at reduced conditions.

Corrosion rate is computed as (nominal thickness minus measured thickness)
divided by years in service. Remaining life is the margin above the design
minimum divided by the corrosion rate.

## 4. Bearing temperature limits
Alarm setpoint for outboard and inboard bearings is 75 degrees Celsius.
Trip setpoint is 90 degrees Celsius. Any reading between alarm and trip
requires lubrication review and vibration analysis within 72 hours.

## 5. Mechanical seal leakage
Weeping up to 2 drops per minute is acceptable for continued operation with
monitoring. Between 2 and 10 drops per minute requires seal replacement at
the next planned opportunity. Above 10 drops per minute requires immediate
shutdown.

## 6. Shutdown window
Once HIGH criticality is assigned, shutdown must be scheduled within 30 days
of the inspection report date. Extension beyond 30 days requires written
deviation approval from the Head of Operations.
