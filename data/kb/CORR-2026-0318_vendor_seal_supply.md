# Internal Correspondence: Mechanical Seal Supply, Crude Feed Pumps

Reference CORR-2026-0318 | From Procurement to Mechanical Maintenance
Date 18 March 2026

## 1. Background
Following the seal failures recorded on P-101A and P-101B during the last
two inspection cycles, Procurement has concluded the framework agreement
with the approved seal vendor for John Crane Type 5610 cartridge seals.

## 2. Seal lead times
Lead time for standard cartridge seals is 21 days from purchase order.
Emergency supply is available at 7 days, with a 35 percent price premium.

## 3. Order terms
Minimum order quantity is two units per size. The agreement covers pump
sizes in the Crude Distillation Unit only.

## 4. Impact on shutdown scheduling
Maintenance Planning should note the 21-day standard lead time when
scheduling shutdowns. Where SOP-MECH-014 requires shutdown within 30 days
of a HIGH criticality finding, the purchase order must be raised within
9 days of the inspection report to avoid breaching the shutdown window.

## 5. Casing replacement
Casing replacements are not covered by this agreement and remain subject to
individual enquiry. Historical casing lead times have run between 10 and
14 weeks, which must be factored into any shutdown plan requiring casing
renewal.
