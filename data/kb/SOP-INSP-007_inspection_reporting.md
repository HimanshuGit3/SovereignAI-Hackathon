# SOP-INSP-007: Equipment Inspection Reporting and Escalation
Revision 3 | Inspection Department | Effective 15 January 2026

## 1. Report numbering
Inspection reports use the format INSP-YYYY-NNNN where YYYY is the calendar
year and NNNN is a sequential number reset each January.

## 2. Mandatory report contents
Every inspection report must record: equipment tag per ISA-5.1, unit,
inspection date, inspector name and certification level, itemised findings,
recommendation, assigned criticality, and next inspection date.

## 3. Criticality assignment
- HIGH: any measurement outside design limits, or any finding affecting
  pressure containment, or equipment in hydrocarbon service with a leak.
- MEDIUM: measurements within limits but trending adversely, or protective
  devices degraded.
- LOW: cosmetic or housekeeping findings with no effect on integrity.

## 4. Approval note requirements
An approval note raised from an inspection report must cite the source report
number, restate measured values exactly as recorded without rounding, state
the recommendation, and identify the approving authority per SOP-MECH-014
section 2.

Paraphrasing of measured values is not permitted. Figures must be transcribed
verbatim from the inspection record.

## 5. Next inspection interval
Default interval is 12 months. Equipment assigned HIGH criticality moves to a
3-month interval until two consecutive satisfactory inspections are recorded.

## 6. Guard and fastener findings
Missing guards or fasteners on rotating equipment are safety findings. They
must be rectified before the equipment is returned to service, irrespective
of the overall criticality assigned.
